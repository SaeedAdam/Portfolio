Hi, I'm **Saeed Adam**, a Senior Software Engineer based in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia. I've been building production web applications since 2021, and today I focus on reliable, maintainable enterprise applications and backend systems.

### What I Do

Most of my work is on the backend: designing APIs and services with **ASP.NET Core**, modelling complex business workflows with **Domain-Driven Design**, and working with **SQL Server**, **PostgreSQL**, **Redis** and **Docker**, shipped through **Azure DevOps**. On the frontend I build with **Angular**, **Vue.js** and **Nuxt**.

Along the way I've worked on enterprise telecom applications at solutions by stc, modular ABP Framework platforms at Kafaat Business Solutions, and enterprise backend services at Ebttikar Technology Company, where I'm currently a Senior Software Engineer.

### How I Work

I think like a product-minded engineer. Before I write code I want to understand the user, the business problem and the outcome we're after, and then pick the simplest solution that actually solves it. I care about clean architecture and SOLID principles because they keep systems easy to change, not as an end in themselves.
